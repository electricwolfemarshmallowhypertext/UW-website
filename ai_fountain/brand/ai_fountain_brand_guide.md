# AI Fountain: Brand Identity Guide

## 🎨 Visual Identity Concept
The AI Fountain brand is built on the intersection of **fluidity (the stream)** and **structure (the network)**. 

### Core Logo
![AI Fountain Logo Concept](file:///home/brad/.gemini/antigravity/brain/65cae12b-04a7-478f-aa35-dbcca9e1ac80/ai_fountain_logo_concept_1776857763017.png)

### Color Palette
*   **Deep Space (Primary)**: `#0B0E14` (Backgrounds, depth)
*   **Electric Stream (Accent)**: `#00D4FF` (Interactive elements, highlights)
*   **Circuit Grey**: `#A1A6B1` (Secondary text, borders)
*   **Neural Glow**: Gradient from `#0082FF` to `#00D4FF`

### Typography
*   **Headlines**: *Inter* or *Outfit* (Bold, high contrast)
*   **Body**: *Roboto Mono* or *Inter* (Clean, legible, technical feel)

---

## 🎭 Tone of Voice
*   **Authoritative**: We know the tech.
*   **Accessible**: We don't use jargon unless we're explaining it.
*   **Participatory**: We don't just speak at the audience; we speak *with* them.

---

## 📐 Application Examples
*   **YouTube Thumbnails**: Dark background, Electric Stream glow around guest faces, bold Circuit Grey text.
*   **Newsletter Header**: Minimalist logo on top-left, neural-glow horizontal divider.
*   **Blog Feature Images**: Glassmorphic cards with Electric Stream shadows.
