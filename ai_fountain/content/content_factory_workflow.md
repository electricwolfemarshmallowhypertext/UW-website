# AI Fountain: "Content Factory" Workflow

To maintain a consistent weekly rhythm without burnout, we will use a **modular production pipeline**.

## 1. Editorial Phase (SiskoAI + AI Agents)
*   **Automated Research**: AI agents scrape the 7 Pillars.
*   **Briefing**: SiskoAI distills the noise into 3 "Bullet Point" triggers for Bradley.
*   **Bradley's Input**: 5-minute review of the brief.

## 2. Production Phase (Lean "Drop-In")
*   **Bradley's Role**: Record a 15-20 min high-level commentary session.
*   **SiskoAI Role**: Record the "News Grind" segments separately (or use AI voice/avatar).
*   **Assembly**: SiskoAI stitches Bradley's "Truth" segments into the broader news episode.

## 3. Repurposing Phase (Thu)
*   **Blog/SEO**: Use an AI agent to transcribe the video and draft a "Deep Dive" blog post for the Ghost site.
*   **Newsletter**: Summarize the news and the episode into the "AI Fountain Weekly" email.
*   **Community**: Post a "Discussion Prompt" on the forum related to the anchor topic.

## 4. Distribution Phase (Fri)
*   **Blast**: Publish simultaneously across all Distributed Channels (YT, Spotify, X, LinkedIn).
*   **Engagement**: Spend 60 minutes responding to comments across YouTube and the Forum.

---

## 🛠️ The "Agentic" Secret Sauce
> [!TIP]
> Do not transcribe or summarize manually. Use an agentic workflow: 
> `Video → Whisper (Transcribe) → GPT (Summarize/Extract Highlights) → Ghost API (Draft Post)`.

---

## ✅ Quality Gate (The "Fountain" Standard)
1.  Is it **accessible** (no unexplained jargon)?
2.  Is it **safe-hosted** (archived on the site before YT launch)?
3.  Does it have a **SiskoAI/Bates CTA**?
